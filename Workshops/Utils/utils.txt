# How It Works

# Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana

## Kibana Is Your Front End And Window.

### Open A Browser And Navigate To '127.0.0.1:5601' And Use Your Credentials To Login. Username: elastic Password As It Appears In .env
### Upon Logging In You Will Be In The Default Workspace, Up In The Top Left Next To The Logo, You Can Create A Custom Workspace.
### It Seems Intimidating, It Just Looks Busy, On The Top Of The Page In The Search Bar Start Typing 'Fleet', The Fleet Server Will Be Your Centralized Agent Management Interface.
### Here You Can Set Up And Dedicate A Fleet Server That Your Deployed Agents Will Communicate With, As Well As Manage Agent Policies, Updates, Etc.
