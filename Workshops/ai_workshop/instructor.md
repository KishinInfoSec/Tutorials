# Instructor Notes & Delivery Strategies

## Emphasize Architecture: Continuously reinforce that the AI is writing the syntax; the attendee's job is to define the architecture and the prompts.

## Content Repurposing: If you record this session, the resulting video and screen shares can easily be segmented into short, high-value technical demonstrations or educational clips for your channels.

## Pro-Tips for Delivering the Workshop

## Visual Checkpoints: Beginners get lost in the terminal. Use architectural diagrams at the start of each module so they know exactly where they are in the factory pipeline.

## The "Fall Forward" Rule: If someone's code breaks, use it as a live teaching moment. Show them how to paste the error into OpenCode or use Claude Code to diagnose the environment. Teaching them how to fix it with AI is more valuable than giving them the right answer.
